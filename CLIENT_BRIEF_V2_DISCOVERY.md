# Client Brief v2 — Discovery Logic

> Source reviewed: **IBM OTM Implementation Discovery Questionnaire**. The source is an implementation-discovery instrument, not a sales scorecard and not an investment-case calculator.

## What is reusable

The source groups discovery into four useful families: the existing systems environment, intended operating outcomes, the people who must collaborate, and a bounded first scope. Its long lists of ERP brands, middleware products, transport modes, regions and document types are important implementation detail, but they are not appropriate for a client’s first three-minute decision conversation.

| Reusable client decision signal | Evidence in the source questionnaire | What the new diagnostic should ask |
|---|---|---|
| **Operating need** | Objectives include planning, carrier management, visibility, settlement, service, cost and legacy replacement. | Is there a real transport problem that the team wants to improve first? |
| **Meaningful operating complexity** | Scope includes inbound/outbound flows, fleet, modes, regions and import/export activity. | Can the team name a flow, region or operating population where the problem is visible? |
| **Digital foundation** | Existing TMS, ERP, integration, data lake/reporting and EDI are collected. | Can the team identify the systems and information that support that starting point? |
| **Collaboration readiness** | Team structure, user groups and learning capability are collected. | Can a business owner and a technology/data owner join the next conversation? |
| **Decision focus** | Go-live window, implementation model and methodology are collected. | Can the organisation agree on a small next exploration, without committing to deployment? |

## What must stay out of a first client diagnostic

Brand-level system inventories, implementation methodology preferences, user counts, exhaustive geographic lists and document-by-document scope collection should be deferred to a guided discovery session. They answer **how to implement**; the client-facing experience first needs to answer **whether OTM deserves a closer look, and where to start**.

## Product boundary for Client Brief v2

Client Brief v2 will be a **three-minute OTM exploration check**, not an ROI calculator, product recommendation, implementation questionnaire or readiness score. It will use six plain-language prompts to help a customer locate their next conversation across three signals: **operating need**, **digital foundation**, and **mobilisation ability**. The output will be a visual “exploration map” and one clear next action, not a numeric score, traffic-light result, ROI claim or buying recommendation.

## Special-industry treatment

The source form establishes that sector-specific requirements often appear through transport modes, private fleet, import/export, compliance and document obligations. Client Brief v2 will therefore treat these as an optional **“what makes your operation different?”** exploration prompt after the universal diagnostic, rather than assuming a generic operating model or forcing specialised questions on every client.
