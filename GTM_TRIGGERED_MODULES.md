# Common Entry and Triggered GTM Modules

## Product decision

The Decision Archive has one common entry: the customer names the operating question that is most worth clarifying. The OTM first route remains **运输计划与装载优化（Shipment Optimization）**. GTM does not appear as a parallel default questionnaire because international-trade work is relevant only when a customer has a defined cross-border, customs, duty, origin or trade-document situation.

## GTM trigger question

> **你的运输或贸易问题，是否涉及跨境申报、原产地／商品归类、关税／FTA、贸易文件，或贸易合规风险？**

If the answer is “not currently,” the customer remains in the OTM path without being asked irrelevant trade questions. If the answer is “yes” or “not sure,” the archive offers the following four short modules; each starts with a concrete operating situation, two evidence prompts and one internal discussion brief.

| Priority module | Use when the customer is trying to clarify | First information to locate |
|---|---|---|
| **商品归类与原产地** | Classification, origin, product attributes or inconsistent master data create manual work or declaration risk. | Product list, current classifications/origin records, and the colleague responsible for master data. |
| **贸易文件与协同** | Documents are late, manually assembled or require repeated follow-up across parties. | A representative shipment file, the documents involved, and the exception/hand-off trail. |
| **申报与代理协同** | Broker, customs-filing or declaration work lacks visibility, control or a clear error trail. | Filing volumes, broker workflow, exception or rework examples. |
| **Landed Cost、FTA 与采购情景** | Duty, FTA eligibility or sourcing alternatives affect supplier or country decisions. | Product/country flow, duty treatment, and origin/FTA eligibility evidence. |

## Do not create a default deep module yet

Duty Drawback and restricted-party screening remain **conditional specialist modules**. They should appear only when the customer specifically identifies recoverable duties, exports tied to imports, sanctioned-party exposure or transaction-control requirements. They are high-consequence and need legal, customs or compliance ownership; a generic customer questionnaire would imply a level of advice the interface should not make.

## Language decision

The Chinese primary label is **运输计划与装载优化**. The first Chinese presentation will show **运输计划与装载优化（Shipment Optimization）** to connect local language with Oracle terminology; later labels in the same route will use the Chinese name alone. English retains “Shipment Optimization”; Spanish uses “Optimización de envíos y cargas.”
