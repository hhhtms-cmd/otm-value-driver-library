# Shipment Optimization — Primary OTM Entry

## Product correction

Shipment Optimization is the primary way a customer should first understand OTM. It is where transport planning becomes operationally different: the organisation can plan orders, capacity, routes, modes and service promises together rather than deciding shipment by shipment through manual judgement.

Freight Audit remains a useful second path because it can often be validated quickly, but it should not be the product’s first explanation of OTM. It answers whether spend and settlement are accurate; Shipment Optimization answers whether the transport plan itself can be made materially better.

## First customer task

> **Shipment Optimization Discussion Brief:** prepare a 45-minute internal discussion to determine where planning is currently leaving cost, capacity, service or planner time on the table.

| Task step | What the customer does | Smallest useful input | What they get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Name the planning constraint** | Choose whether the practical pain is underfilled loads, manual route/mode decisions, fragmented orders, or frequent expedites. | One recurring planning situation. | A bounded improvement question. |
| **2. Locate the planning trail** | Confirm which orders, shipment history, rate/service choices and constraints can be reviewed. | One sample period or a representative lane/flow. | A focused information checklist. |
| **3. Bring the right operating view** | Involve the people who understand transport planning, service commitments and cost/rate choices. | Two or three knowledgeable colleagues. | A 45-minute internal agenda and discussion brief. |

## Customer language

The primary route should use plain operating language: **plan fuller loads, make better route and service choices, reduce avoidable expedites, and free planners from repetitive decisions**. It should not promise savings, require ROI, or claim a solution fit before information has been reviewed.

## Runway order

1. **Shipment Optimization** — OTM / 01; primary route.
2. **Freight Spend Integrity** — OTM / 04; secondary path for invoice, rate and settlement questions.
3. **Visibility and Exceptions** — OTM / 05; secondary path for service and response questions.
4. **Other transport or trade question** — routes to the full library only after the customer names a specific issue.

## Evidence boundary

The initial Shipment Optimization brief prepares an internal conversation. It does not calculate savings. A value estimate remains available only after the customer can substantiate a baseline, relevant planning constraints, and the scope to which the change applies.
